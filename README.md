# Réda Ouzzane

I am an MVA master's student (Mathematics, Vision and Learning)
at ENS Paris-Saclay, with an engineering background from Centrale Lille.

I am looking for a research internship starting in April 2027,
with an interest in pursuing a PhD afterwards.

My research interests include diffusion models, flow matching,
optimal transport and generative modeling.

## Research experience

**Numerical inversion of diffusion models — CNRS, CRIStAL Lab**

I studied the numerical reversibility of latent diffusion models
and the influence of ODE solvers and sampling settings on reconstruction.
This project was supervised by Patrick Bas, Jan Butora and Aurélien Noirault.

**Reward-guided sampling — Personal research project**

I implemented and compared full-gradient Tweedie guidance,
an isotropic covariance approximation and LiDAR for Stable Diffusion.
I examined trade-offs between luminance reward, learned evaluation
metrics and computational cost.
